About us

A passion for moments that stay with you

There is a moment at every great gathering when the room settles in. Glasses are raised, conversations deepen, and nobody is thinking about the time. Creating that moment is what we love to do, and it is why hospitality sits at the center of everything at Stirring Up. We plan each event around its moments, from the first welcome to the final toast, because those moments turn into memories that last a lifetime. You cannot put a price tag on an experience like that. It is priceless in the truest sense, and it deserves care long before the first guest arrives.

What makes an evening unforgettable is rarely one big gesture. It is attention to detail in a hundred small ones. The right drink at the right moment. A bar that feels calm and ready. A guest who does not drink alcohol being handed something just as thoughtful. That is the hospitality we bring to every wedding, celebration, and corporate gathering we pour for.

Alejandro

The mixologist

Alejandro brings over five years of experience behind the bar in upscale dining, where the standard is high and every detail counts. That time taught him how to read a room, pace an evening well, and craft a drink that suits the person standing in front of him.

More than technique, it gave him a love for creating experiences worth repeating. Guests come back for a good cocktail. They remember, and return for, how they were made to feel.
